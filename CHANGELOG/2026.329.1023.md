# 2026.329.1023 — feat(rulebook): add R12 — spike every candidate solution before implementing

## Summary

The validator's committed rulebook (`AI_REVIEW_PROMPT` in `charly.yml`) gains **R12 — spike every candidate solution before implementing**, the new spine rule of opencharly/opencharly#1278. After an RCA names the cause, the author lists every plausible solution for it and spikes each one. The diff implements the candidate that works, and that candidate's success is the RCA's proof. If none works, the RCA is wrong.

| hunk | change |
|---|---|
| A1 (`How tested` item) | names the `### Spike` block whenever R12 applies |
| section B header | `R0–R11` → `R0–R12` |
| R1 | new bullet: an RCA the change acts on is proved by a working R12 candidate |
| R12 (new block, before section C) | BLOCKS on a missing `### Spike` block, on a single spiked candidate with no other named, on implementing something other than the winner or after a round where nothing worked, on a docs-only "spike", and on an unnamed or inapplicable exemption; a spike never replaces R10 |
| PIL / RDD | no longer gated on "if a HIGH-RISK assumption is touched": a fix's round is verified under R12, and a high-risk assumption still needs its bed |
